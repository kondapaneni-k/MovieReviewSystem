1. How do I submit a movie review?
To submit a movie review, log in to your account, navigate to the movie page, and click the "Write a Review" button. Share your thoughts and click "Submit."
2. Can I edit my review after submission?
Yes, you can edit your review within 24 hours of submission. Navigate to your profile, find the review, and click "Edit."
3. How do I create an account?
Click on the "Sign Up" button on the top right corner of the homepage, provide your details, and follow the instructions to create an account.
4. How do I find the latest movie reviews?
The latest movie reviews are featured on our homepage under the "Trending Reviews" section. You can also use the search bar to look for specific movies.
5. Can I filter movies by genre?
Yes, you can use the "Genres" dropdown menu on the homepage to filter movies by categories such as Action, Comedy, Drama, and more.
6. How do I rate a movie?
To rate a movie, visit the movie's page, select your desired star rating, and confirm your rating by clicking "Submit Rating."
7. Can I see reviews from other users?
Yes, you can view user reviews by visiting a movie's page and scrolling to the "User Reviews" section.
8. How do I contact customer support?
You can contact our customer support team through the "Contact Us" page on our website or via email at support@cineazure.com.
9. Does CINE AZURE have subscription?
There is no subscription plan users can enjoy all the features
10. How do I report inappropriate content?
To report inappropriate content, click the "Report" button located under the review or comment. Our team will review the report and take appropriate action.
